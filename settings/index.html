1. Open home.nest.com in Chrome and sign in with Google.
  2. Open DevTools (F12) → Network, tick Preserve log, then reload.
  3. Filter for issueToken. Copy the full Request URL into the first field.
  4. Filter for oauth2/iframe. Under Request Headers, copy the whole cookie value into the second field.
  5. Close the tab, but do not sign out — signing out invalidates the cookie.
