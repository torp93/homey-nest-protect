1. Open https://home.nest.com in Chrome and sign in with Google.
  2. Open DevTools (F12), go to Network, and tick Preserve log.
  3. Filter for issueToken. Open the request, copy the full Request URL into the first field.
  4. Filter for oauth2/iframe. In Request Headers, copy the entire cookie value into the second field.
  5. Close the tab, but do not sign out of Google — signing out invalidates the cookie.
